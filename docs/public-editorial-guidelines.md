# Editorial Guidelines

*The Unplanned Show: Standards & Practices*

**Effective immediately, pending further review.**

The Unplanned Show is an independent satirical publication covering urban planning, planning education, academia, and the extraordinary human effort required to accomplish very little.

Our editorial standards are rigorous, arbitrary, and subject to change without public engagement.

## Article I. Word Limits

No news article shall exceed **500 words**.

Articles exceeding this limit will be referred to the Committee on Unnecessary Elaboration, which does not exist.

Shorter articles are encouraged. Articles containing no useful information may receive priority consideration.

## Article II. Interviews

Interviews shall contain **no more than two questions**.

The first question may be reasonable.

The second is at the discretion of the editor.

Follow-up questions require a comprehensive planning process and will therefore not be permitted.

## Article III. Evidence and Accuracy

Facts must be accurate unless clearly presented as fiction or satire.

Statistics may be ridiculous but must not be falsely attributed to real studies.

Real people shall not be assigned imaginary quotations.

We reserve the right to misunderstand planning terminology for comedic purposes.

## Article IV. Editorial Independence

The Editor-in-Chief retains final editorial authority.

Editorial decisions may be appealed to the Editor-in-Chief.

Appeals will be reviewed by the same Editor-in-Chief.

## Article V. Submissions

We welcome contributions from students, alumni, faculty, practitioners, and anyone who has attended a meeting that could have been an email.

Submissions need not demonstrate practical relevance, methodological innovation, or a clear contribution to the literature.

A sense of humor is preferred.

## Article VI. Conflicts of Interest

Contributors must disclose conflicts of interest, including:

- Membership in more than three planning committees.
- Personal involvement in the comprehensive plan being mocked.
- Prior use of the phrase "robust stakeholder engagement" without irony.

## Article VII. Public Engagement

Public comments are welcome.

They will be carefully documented, summarized, and filed for future consideration.

No changes are guaranteed.

## Article VIII. Corrections

Factual errors will be corrected.

Unsuccessful jokes may be quietly removed.

Successful jokes will be attributed to the editorial team.

## Final Provision

These guidelines shall be reviewed periodically, or whenever someone remembers they exist.

**No comprehensive plan was prepared in the development of these guidelines.**

*The Editorial Office*
*The Unplanned Show*

Adopted unanimously by an editorial board of one.
