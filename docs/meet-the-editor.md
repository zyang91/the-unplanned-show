**Meet Zhanchao Yang, MCP & MUSA**

*Founder & Editor-in-Chief*
*Chair of the Editorial Board*

Zhanchao Yang holds a Master of City Planning and a Master of Urban Spatial Analytics from the Weitzman School of Design at the University of Pennsylvania (26). He is also a recipient of the C. Britton Harris Award for excellence in transportation planning.

During his graduate studies, he diligently attended studio reviews, drafted urgent memos on matters with limited urgency, and participated in site visits that confirmed the existence of the sites. He produced numerous maps of things already known, developed statistical models to explain the obvious patterns, and attended networking happy hours that resulted in neither networking nor happiness. His academic papers identified several research gaps, some of which he had personally created.

Having spent an unusually long time in planning school than his peers, Yang eventually earned two master's degrees while retaining only a limited understanding of what planning actually is and continue doubt about it. 

Despite holding a MUSA degree and TA for Python, R, and statistics four times, he still struggle to identify the different between causation and correlation. He vibe coded multiple applications and assignment and leave behind a substantial portfolio of software, no one is interested in maintaining. 

The University nevertheless certified him as qualified.

He founded *The Unplanned Show* to investigate this discrepancy without preparing another memo.

Yang currently serves as Founder, Editor-in-Chief, and Chair of the Editorial Board. All appointments were approved unanimously.
