# Editorial policy and disclaimer

## Satire and fact

The Unplanned Show is an independent satirical publication. Exaggeration, parody and invented scenarios are part of its method. Readers should assume everything they read here is satire unless it is listed under "Sources and references" at the foot of an article; documented material is cited there.

## Images

Images on this site may be illustrations, composites, edited or AI-generated. No image is guaranteed to show a real person, place or event as it happened, and none is offered as evidence. Captions are part of the joke unless a source is cited.

## Independence

The publication does not claim to represent a university, government agency, employer or professional association. Views expressed are those of the publication alone.

## Editorial standards

- Substantive factual claims are verified before publication.
- Fictional quotations are clearly distinguished from real statements.
- Statistics are not fabricated, and invented results are never presented as empirical findings.
- References are provided when an article relies on documented events, research or institutional records.
- Criticism is directed at institutional practices, not at unsupported allegations about individuals.
- Irony, exaggeration, parody and self-deprecating humor are welcome.
- An article is not required to propose a policy solution.

## Working titles

Planned topics are satirical concepts, not factual allegations or completed findings. Planned topics are not published as stories.

## Version 1 features

There is no advertising, no paywall, no reader accounts and no comments.
